abstract: >-
  Class imbalance limits the accuracy of credit prediction for smallholder farmers.
  We propose a dynamic cost-adjusted AdaCost model in which the cost-sensitive
  function is updated jointly with the sample weights during boosting, rather than
  being fixed in advance as in conventional AdaCost. Experiments show that the
  proposed model outperforms both AdaBoost and the original AdaCost.
