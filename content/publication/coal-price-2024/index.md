abstract: >-
  We propose a window-rolling ICEEMDAN decomposition-ensemble framework for
  forecasting the Bohai-Rim Steam-Coal Price Index, combining decomposition with
  ANN, CNN, LSTM and GRU predictors. Rolling the decomposition window avoids
  information leakage and boundary effects, and the resulting models improve both
  accuracy and stability over other rolling baselines.
