## About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Texas at Arlington (UTA), where I work in the [MIND Lab](https://ranger.uta.edu/~zhu) under the supervision of [Prof. Dajiang Zhu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=cFgudIYAAAAJ&hl=en). I received my B.Sc. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Tianjin University of Commerce in 2023.

Before starting my Ph.D., I spent two and a half years in industry as an AI algorithm engineer. At the Tencent & Canway Joint R&D Team, I built graph neural network models for root cause analysis and Transformer-based models for time series forecasting and anomaly detection. Earlier, at Multisim, I worked on deep learning for time series forecasting, large sparse solvers for simulation, and Bayesian optimization for turbulence model calibration.

My current research lies at the intersection of machine learning and neuroimaging, with a focus on hyperbolic representation learning, multimodal deep learning, and disease progression modeling for Alzheimer's disease. I am always happy to discuss research and potential collaborations.
